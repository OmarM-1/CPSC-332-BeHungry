Purpose (Azaan Mavandadipur):
The Recipe Sharing Platform is designed to address the fragmented nature of online food culture by creating a centralized, intuitive, and community-driven hub for culinary enthusiasts. Its primary purpose is to empower users to both share their own culinary creations and browse a diverse library of recipes submitted by others. In a landscape often cluttered with scattered blogs and ad-heavy sites, this platform will provide a clean, dedicated space for discovering and exchanging dishes. By integrating core features like user login, structured recipe submission, a robust search function, and a community-focused comment and rating system, the platform will foster an interactive and collaborative environment where users can connect, discuss, and find inspiration, transforming the solitary act of finding a recipe into a shared social experience.

Introduction (Omar Montes):
Nowadays we have so many apps that are made to distract from reality and entertain. What we lack is useful tools that can educate and help grow people. Our take on a social media app is to keep it on the basis of culinary arts. We want to have people scroll in the same way they do on social media but instead of lifestyle, we want impressions on other people's plates and how they make food. We believe the concept could teach a lot of people that want to learn how to create a healthier lifestyle for themselves. Users are going to be able to like, share and read about new recipes and how to make them. We hope to encourage feedback and collective learning rather than distracting and wasting time. We hope to engage people to learn how to cook for themselves, try new things, and be proud of it rather than going the simpler route and buying fast food or spending on going out to eat at restaurants. Whether it’s an amazing take on a quesadilla or grandma’s famous casserole, it should spark new ideas to the average user. 
Features (Moises Munoz):
The recipe sharing platform will consist of the following features: user login, recipe submission, recipe searches and a comment system. Users will be able create an account to login and either search for recipes or submit their own. Recipe submission will allow any user to be able to submit a recipe of their choice along with the necessary ingredients and steps. Recipe search will allow users to be able to search for specific recipes. The comment system will allow users to provide feedback on a given recipe and even share their cooking experiences with other people.

Functional Requirements (Derrick Nghe):
User Management and Profiles:
FR-01: Users shall be able to create accounts using email, phone number, or third-party login.
FR-02: Users shall be able to edit and manage their profiles.
Recipe Submission and Management:
FR-03: Users shall be able to create and submit recipes.
FR-04: Users shall be able to edit or delete their own submitted recipes.
Search and Browsing:
FR-05: Users shall be able to search for recipes by name, ingredient, or category. 
FR-06: Users shall be able to browse recipes by popularity, newest, or diet.
Interaction and Community Features
FR-07: Users shall be able to like and comment on recipes.
FR-08: Users shall be able to bookmark or save recipes to their library.
Notifications and Admin control
FR-09: Users shall receive notifications for likes and comments.
FR-10: Admin shall be able to remove inappropriate recipes or comments to maintain community standards.

Non-Functional Requirements (Alec McGee):
Performance Requirements:
The app homepage should load within 2 seconds.
The app should support at least 10000 users at a time without loss in performance.
Security Requirements:
All user data must be encrypted.
User input must be sanitized and validated to prevent misuse.
Compatibility Requirements:
Apps must support the latest IOS and Android versions.
Must allow for user uploaded images, supporting JPEG PNG WEBP file formats.
Reliability Requirements:
The app must maintain a 99.5% uptime.
User data must be stored in 2 separate data centers.
